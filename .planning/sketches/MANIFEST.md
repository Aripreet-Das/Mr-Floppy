# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
"Zero install, on-phone transfer indicator — cool and funky." The phone-side surface splits honestly by ceiling: the notification drawer is system-drawn chrome where funk lives in words, emoji, unicode art, and live numbers (Sketch 001); the phone browser is a full canvas where the app's night-bench aesthetic (obsidian glass + amber LED, JetBrains Mono figures) can breathe (Sketch 002). The notification is the always-on backbone; the browser page is the opt-in "show me" mode.

## Reference Points
- User brief: "cool and funky" (no external references given)
- Desktop app theme in production: node-canvas obsidian glass, amber accent, Plus Jakarta Sans + JetBrains Mono
